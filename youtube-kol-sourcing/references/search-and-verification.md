# Search and verification

## Search strategy

Generate search themes from the product and adjacent categories, buyer persona, pain points and outcomes, allowed competitor or alternative-solution terms, desired creator formats, target markets and languages, and budget tier. Build multilingual queries only for languages requested by the user.

If qualified count is low, expand through direct niche creators, adjacent-problem creators, alternative-solution reviewers, audience communities, use-case educators, regional creators, and smaller creators with strong recent engagement and public contact routes. Do not lower strict gates merely to fill the pool.

## Candidate verification

For each candidate:

1. Confirm channel identity and creator-led nature.
2. Check account history and note uncertainty.
3. Check ignored-market and competitor risk.
4. Verify the latest relevant upload date and recent topical consistency.
5. Capture the latest ten relevant titles, dates, and views when available.
6. Calculate the latest-ten average and latest-six average and median; enforce strict gates unless the user authorized a fallback.
7. Judge content quality, audience fit, demo ability, visible persona, and buyer relevance.
8. Deduplicate by normalized creator or channel name against prior lists.
9. Apply explicit competitor-reference exclusions.
10. Check contact readiness and record the exact source.
11. Choose one partnership angle and explain why it fits the audience.
12. Put only qualified creators into strict final tables.

Use current YouTube evidence wherever possible. Search snippets are for discovery, not final recency, playback, region, or contact proof. Re-verify candidates found in old snapshots and state the snapshot date if it remains relevant.

## Batch expansion

Run one focused batch per high-intent category. Remove obvious exclusions early, verify playback for survivors, then inspect contact routes. Use the largest rejection reasons to design the next batch. Stop when the target is met, the user stops the task, or the market is clearly exhausted under the current rules; report the exact shortfall and strongest remaining search directions.

## Deduplication

Do not modify source sheets. Locate creator-name columns in outreach, cooperation, candidate, or similar sheets. Normalize case, punctuation, handles, and simple suffixes such as `official`, `channel`, `podcast`, or `show`. Remove matches from recommendations and record matched source, name, row or record when available, action, and reason in `Dedupe Audit`.

Use a local export only when live access fails and a fresh export exists. Label it as cache-based or stale; never claim live deduplication.

## Comment review

Review comments only after the main sourcing gates. Sample three to five recent relevant videos and 50–100 comments per video when tooling permits. Look for questions and discussion about the campaign problem, use case, alternatives, price, setup, integrations, workflow, results, credibility, or purchase intent. Flag spam, giveaways, bot-like praise, irrelevant intent, and excluded markets. If comments are inaccessible, record the blocker and do not invent findings.

## Common failures

Avoid stale activity evidence, subscriber count as a playback proxy, hidden email buttons marked as verified addresses, inferred or template emails, contact forms labeled as emails, company or podcast channels slipping into strict creator pools, duplicate creators presented as new, and conditional candidates presented as strict recommendations.
